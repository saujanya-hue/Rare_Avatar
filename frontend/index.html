🎲 Aleatoric Uncertainty (Biological Variance)

Represents intrinsic natural patient-to-patient heterogeneity, diurnal physiological fluctuations, and analytical instrument precision tolerances.

  • Reported via explicit 95% confidence intervals around continuous biomarker levels.
  • Derived from empirical multi-center cohort variance parameters in published registries.

📚 Epistemic Uncertainty (Evidence Density)

Represents boundaries in medical knowledge, sparse clinical literature for rare phenotypes, and degrees of consensus among clinical guidelines.

  • Scored quantitatively (0.0 to 1.0) based on peer-reviewed evidence level: Level A (guidelines/RCTs), Level B (cohort registries), Level C (case series).
  • Displays explicit caveats when evidence is scarce or conflicting.
